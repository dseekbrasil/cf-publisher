MyKasih Explained: Malaysia's Smart Food Aid System

Published October 19, 2026

Understanding MyKasih

MyKasih is a Malaysian charitable foundation that delivers food aid to low-income families in a smart, cashless way. Rather than giving out physical money, it loads aid onto a family's MyKad (their national ID card), which is then used at approved shops.

Why the cashless model matters

This approach keeps help dignified and targeted. A family simply taps their MyKad at the till of a participating store, and the aid is spent only on approved essentials like food and groceries. There is no cash to lose or misuse.

Who benefits

The programme is built for B40 households, the lowest-income group in Malaysia, as well as families facing hardship. The same technology has also supported large government welfare efforts reaching many thousands of households.

Beyond groceries

The foundation also invests in education and community projects, from supporting schools to local livelihood programmes, helping families build a steadier future.
